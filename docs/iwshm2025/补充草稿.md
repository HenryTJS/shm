## 补充 A —— 引言末尾（对应意见 1）

> Multi-institution records are already being used for more than comparison. As monitoring networks grow beyond single structures, the diagnostic knowledge obtained on one asset is expected to carry to nominally similar ones, whether the records are pooled across an asset fleet or transferred from a source structure to a target for which no labelled damage state exists [refs: IWSHM 1876, 2513, 2189, 1817]. Each of those uses presupposes that the records are commensurable in the first place, and the practice of publishing shared monitoring datasets exists precisely because that presupposition is otherwise hard to test [ref: IWSHM 610]. The protocol below is what establishes the commensurability; it is a precondition for such transfer, not a substitute for it.

【说明】三处引用可按 IWSHM 2025 的对应论文换算成你最终要投的版本。末句的「precondition, not substitute」是定位句，建议保留原样。

## 补充 B —— 早锚定的代价（对应意见 2，建议单列一小节）

> SNHI is defined against the value the record reaches at its end, which is available only after the record is complete. A natural question is whether an earlier anchor, one available while the structure is still in service, can stand in for it.
>
> The two are not interchangeable, and the reason is not a matter of degree. Asking for the lifetime fraction at which the curve reaches $x$ per cent of a partial total is a different question from asking for the same fraction of the final total: the answer is bounded above by the anchor position by construction, so the resulting number is not a re-estimate of the same quantity but a different one. Re-anchoring therefore cannot be validated by comparing the two positions directly.
>
> The empirical question that remains is how much of the final total has already accumulated when the anchor is taken. Table X reports the ratio $C(f)/C(\text{end})$ at five anchor positions for the 22 records whose cumulative curves are available.
>
> | Anchor $f$ | Median share | Range | Spread across specimens | CV |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | 0.05 | 0.027 | 0.002 to 0.599 | 273 times | 2.14 |
> | 0.10 | 0.058 | 0.005 to 0.617 | 118 times | 1.49 |
> | 0.20 | 0.127 | 0.020 to 0.647 | 32 times | 0.89 |
> | 0.30 | 0.208 | 0.031 to 0.681 | 22 times | 0.63 |
> | 0.50 | 0.361 | 0.153 to 0.758 | 5.0 times | 0.38 |
>
> At a fifth of life the accumulated share differs between specimens by a factor of more than thirty, and even at mid-life it still differs by a factor of five. No fixed correction factor can compensate for a spread of that size, which is itself larger than the between-batch spread that the protocol is able to resolve. Early anchoring is therefore admissible only if the end value can be forecast from the partial record, and the accuracy of such a forecast, rather than the choice of anchor, becomes the quantity that governs the usable lead time.
>
> This is why the indicator is presented as a retrospective descriptor. It does not follow that the protocol is unusable in service, but the usable quantity in that setting is the relative position of one specimen against another on the same axis, which is unaffected by a common anchor error, and not the absolute lifetime fraction.

【说明】最后一句是给意见 2 的实质回答，同时给出了一条在役可用的退路（相对排序），与 IWSHM_2189 的做法同构。若要进一步强化，可补一句「排序在何处失效」的边界条件。

## 补充 C —— 与指标排名方法的关系（对应意见 3）

> Monotonicity, trendability and prognosability were introduced to rank candidate indicators [Saxena et al. 2010]. SNHI does not compete with them; it is one of the candidates they score, and the relationship is that of an object to an instrument.
>
> Applying the metrics to SNHI does, however, expose a degeneracy that is worth stating. Because SNHI is read off the self-normalised cumulative curve, that curve is monotone by construction, and two such curves are necessarily highly correlated. Measured on the 22 available records, the median monotonicity and the median pairwise trendability of the cumulative curves are both 1.000, which carries no information. The metrics become informative only when applied to the rate signal that generates the curve: there the median monotonicity falls to 0.430 and the median pairwise trendability to 0.131, with the weakest pair at −0.594. Prognosability is the one of the three that remains meaningful on the cumulative curve itself, at 0.919, reflecting a spread of the reported position from 67.9% to 99.1% of life about a median of 93.1%.
>
> The reading we take is therefore narrower than a claim of superiority. SNHI is well posed as an object for these metrics, and its insensitivity to instrument gain and threshold follows from the same construction that makes the cumulative curve monotone. Whether it ranks above alternative indicators on trendability is a separate question, and on the rate signal it does not: the median trendability of 0.131 is low. That limitation is accepted rather than argued away, and it is the reason the protocol is framed around comparability of position rather than around the quality of the indicator as a trend detector.

【说明】这一段比审稿人要的「complement not replace」更强：它承认在速率口径下趋势性只有 0.131，偏低。主动承认这一点反而更可信，也顺手把「为什么本文不靠趋势性好坏来立论」讲通了。
